Startups

Companies founded by SRA alumni.

Elevar Sports

Founded by Rohit Bhaskar

Elevar Sports is a tech-focused sports D2C brand that creates innovative performance footwear and cricket equipment. Founded in 2018 and headquartered in Mumbai, Elevar combines cutting-edge design and engineering to push the boundaries of possibility — from revolutionary materials to designs that craft sneaker icons and sports equipment of the future.

Visit website ↗

Eye Candy Robotics

Founded by Mohd Alqama Shaikh

Eye Candy Robotics is a robotics company founded by a VJTI alumnus and former SRA member. The company focuses on building innovative robotic solutions, combining engineering expertise with creative design to develop cutting-edge products in the robotics space.

Instamojo

Founded by Aditya Sengupta

Instamojo aims to solve one problem – make the internet a trustworthy place for a business to grow. Instamojo started with Payment Links – a simple URL that one can copy and paste anywhere on the internet and receive payments online via any payment mode. Today its an eCommerce platform that allows anyone to open their own online store on the platform in just a few clicks.
